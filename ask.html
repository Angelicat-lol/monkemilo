About Us

    Bello, this is the monke.milo team and here's a bit about us. Our names are Andrew and Angie. Milo is my puppy dawg, he's a German Shepard Pitbull mix and looooves to sleep.

    Angie:

      HAI ^ - ^ I am the founding member of monke.milo. Few know this but monke.milo was not always about the matcha... it was actually a passion project instagram page dedicated to my knitware. I posted all kinds of monke.milo themed chothing for about a year and a half until it unfortunetly got taken down :( Thank you for that tacobell. Monke.milo was abandoned for a year, stopped knitting because it felt like it was taken from me in a way. However in September 2025 my buddy Andrew mentioned wanting to start a matcha business, ofc as a passing thought but everything is a serious topic to me! So I mocked up a proposal and in November 2025 we held our first market in the heart of SanMo. Vanilla Bean. To this day we are still in the negatives; regardless I charish all the memories I have with monke.milo and how far we've come. Lots to do but forever grateful for everyone who's ever purchased any item from us <3

    Andrew:

      Bello, my name is Andrew and I was one of the founders of the matcha side monke.milo.
